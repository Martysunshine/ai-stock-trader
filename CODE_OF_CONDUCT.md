# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions) should be able to
do so without harassment, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Criticise the code or the idea, not the person.
- Say what you know and what you only suspect. In a trading project, a confident wrong claim
  can cost someone money.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and fix them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or sexualised language or imagery.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Promoting a trading product, a signal service or a "guaranteed return" scheme.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do
not follow this code, and may block a participant for behaviour that is threatening, offensive
or harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. For
anything that should stay private, use **Report a vulnerability** under the repository's
Security tab and say that it is a conduct report.
