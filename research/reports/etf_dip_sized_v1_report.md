## 3. Execution and cost assumptions

- Fees: `alpaca-us-equities-conservative-2026-11-08` (Alpaca US equities via API - zero commission; SEC Section 31, FINRA TAF and CAT pass-through at the highest rates in force 2022-01..2026-11, each rounded up to USD 0.01 per order).
- Base fills: half-spread 1.0 bps, slippage 1.0 bps, participation 0.10 of bar volume, latency 500 ms, simulated missed entries 0%.
- Stressed fills: half-spread 2.0 bps, slippage 2.0 bps, participation 0.10 of bar volume, latency 500 ms, simulated missed entries 5%.
- Entries (daily bars, positions carried overnight): decided on a completed session's close; a marketable limit at the estimated ask plus the rule's frozen offset (T-0017 5 bps, at most 10 sessions, target is a cost gate only), filled only at the next session's open within the limit, else missed. Exit signals and time exits sell at the next open; a stop rests for the whole hold, is checked before the target in the same bar, and an open through it sells at that open (STOP_GAP). The target needs the estimated bid strictly through it. The signal bar's close is never a fill price.
- Risk at USD 50 (shared policy): planned loss per trade <= 0.125 USD including both fees and a 10 bps gap allowance; <= 10 USD per symbol, <= 20 USD gross, >= 30 USD cash reserve; at most 2 new entries per NY session and one pending entry; daily 0.50 USD and weekly 1.25 USD loss pauses, 10% drawdown and the USD 45 floor.
- Research assumptions: every 3-month window is an independent deployment from a fresh USD 50 ledger (bars of the 260 preceding exchange sessions feed the features only; no decision before the window is traded; a position still open at the window end is closed at the last bar's estimated bid). Inside a window a daily/weekly loss pause is treated as reviewed at the next loss window, and a drawdown or floor pause stops new entries until the window ends.
- Bar data cannot resolve sub-bar order: these results are preliminary strategy research and do not certify execution realism (risk_and_validation.md §6).

## 12. Bias and limitations

- Universe (declared at registration): The 13 US equity ETFs of T-0013, unchanged: SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA and the nine original Select Sector SPDRs (XLB, XLE, XLF, XLI, XLK, XLP, XLU, XLV, XLY). No fund was added or removed after seeing T-0013's result; keeping the universe is what makes the policy the only change. Today's list applied to past history, with the small survivorship bias described in etf_daily_v1. The funds overlap heavily, and dips arrive together: exposure tags cap SPY, QQQ, IWM and DIA together at the USD 10 symbol cap (US_EQUITY_INDEX), sector funds are untagged, and at most two positions are open. When several funds signal at the same close, the first in instrument-id order is taken.
- Entry, exit and feed (declared at registration): As T-0013 in etf_daily_v1: decision at a completed daily close (+1 s); a next-open marketable buy limit at the estimated ask plus 5 bps, filled only at the next session's open plus the assumed half-spread and slippage, so an open more than about 3 bps above the signal close misses the entry; the exit signal (a close above SMA(5)) and the time exit sell at the next open; the disaster stop rests for the whole hold and an open through it sells at that open; the target (SMA(5) at the signal) is only a cost-coverage gate, never an exit. Larger positions do not change fills: USD 10 orders stay far below the 10% participation cap on these funds. With larger positions the cost gate admits more signals than at T-0013's sizes, because the per-order cent roundings are a smaller fraction of the planned gain; the trade count is therefore expected to differ from T-0013's 55.
- Bar-only replay: no quotes, queue position or intrabar path; the spread is an assumption rather than a measurement, and passive (limit-resting) execution is not modelled.
- Pessimistic intrabar sequencing: when one bar touches both the stop and the target, the stop is assumed (never target-first). Zero-commission runs: T-0017 0 of 246 trades were decided this way, so this bias is small. (Stops inside the entry bar are not an assumption: the entry fills at that bar's open, so its low comes after the entry.)
- Costs: Alpaca US equities via API - zero commission; SEC Section 31, FINRA TAF and CAT pass-through at the highest rates in force 2022-01..2026-11, each rounded up to USD 0.01 per order. Taxes are not modelled; one USD account currency is assumed.
- Loss-pause reviews are assumed at the next window; a real owner might pause longer.
- The walk-forward series is short relative to the G2 floor of 120 effective sessions and 100 trades unless stated otherwise above; uncertainty intervals are wide.
