## 3. Execution and cost assumptions

- Fees: `alpaca-us-equities-conservative-2026-11-08` (Alpaca US equities via API - zero commission; SEC Section 31, FINRA TAF and CAT pass-through at the highest rates in force 2022-01..2026-11, each rounded up to USD 0.01 per order).
- Base fills: half-spread 2.0 bps, slippage 2.0 bps, participation 0.10 of bar volume, latency 500 ms, simulated missed entries 0%.
- Stressed fills: half-spread 4.0 bps, slippage 4.0 bps, participation 0.10 of bar volume, latency 500 ms, simulated missed entries 5%.
- Entries: marketable limit at estimated ask + 5 bps, filled only at the next bar's open within the limit, else missed. Stop is checked before target in the same bar; the target needs the estimated bid strictly through it. The signal bar's close is never a fill price.
- Risk at USD 50 (shared policy): planned loss per trade <= 0.125 USD including both fees and a 10 bps gap allowance; <= 10 USD per symbol, <= 20 USD gross, >= 30 USD cash reserve; at most 2 new entries per NY session and one pending entry; daily 0.50 USD and weekly 1.25 USD loss pauses, 10% drawdown and the USD 45 floor.
- Research assumptions: every 3-month window is an independent deployment from a fresh USD 50 ledger (bars from the two preceding calendar days feed the features, and both stock rules reset their state at every session open; a position still open at the window end is closed at the last bar's estimated bid). Inside a window a daily/weekly loss pause is treated as reviewed at the next loss window, and a drawdown or floor pause stops new entries until the window ends.
- Bar data cannot resolve sub-bar order: these results are preliminary strategy research and do not certify execution realism (risk_and_validation.md §6).

## 12. Bias and limitations

- Universe (declared at registration): Fixed by strategy_and_profitability.md section 3 before any stock data was inspected: AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN and NVDA. This is today's mega-cap list applied to 2022-2026 history, not a point-in-time universe: survivorship and look-ahead selection bias (these names are known to have grown into the largest US companies) and single-sector concentration (five technology-related names) are disclosed, not corrected. No symbol was chosen from results. No point-in-time earnings or corporate-event calendar is available: Candidate B's "no identified company event" condition and the section 3 rule to avoid scheduled earnings windows are NOT enforced, so earnings-day bars are traded.
- Entry, exit and feed (declared at registration): Decision at bar end + 1 s, order live 500 ms later; marketable buy limit at the estimated ask plus 5 bps, filled only at the next bar's open within the limit (TTL 120 s), otherwise missed. Exits: time exit, close procedure and gap-through-stop at the next open; intrabar stop before target when both are touched; target only when the estimated bid trades strictly through it. All fills at estimated bid/ask plus slippage. Bars are Alpaca IEX (one venue): bar volume (the participation cap) and the source session VWAP (the reference of both candidates) are IEX-only, not consolidated. Orders are fractional day limit orders; Alpaca's documentation is inconsistent about fractional limit orders, so their acceptance is an UNVERIFIED assumption until a paper probe (M4). Alpaca zero commission plus SEC, FINRA TAF and CAT pass-through fees (configs/fees/alpaca_us_equities_v2.yaml).
- Bar-only replay: no quotes, queue position or intrabar path; the spread is an assumption rather than a measurement, and passive (limit-resting) execution is not modelled.
- Pessimistic intrabar sequencing: when one bar touches both the stop and the target, the stop is assumed (never target-first). Zero-commission runs: T-0006 0 of 2178 trades; T-0007 0 of 2174 trades; T-0008 0 of 2164 trades; T-0009 0 of 2164 trades; T-0010 1 of 1833 trades were decided this way, so this bias is small. (Stops inside the entry bar are not an assumption: the entry fills at that bar's open, so its low comes after the entry.)
- Costs: Alpaca US equities via API - zero commission; SEC Section 31, FINRA TAF and CAT pass-through at the highest rates in force 2022-01..2026-11, each rounded up to USD 0.01 per order. Taxes are not modelled; one USD account currency is assumed.
- Loss-pause reviews are assumed at the next window; a real owner might pause longer.
- The walk-forward series is short relative to the G2 floor of 120 effective sessions and 100 trades unless stated otherwise above; uncertainty intervals are wide.
