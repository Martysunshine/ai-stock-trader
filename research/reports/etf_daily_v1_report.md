## 3. Execution and cost assumptions

- Fees: `alpaca-us-equities-conservative-2026-11-08` (Alpaca US equities via API - zero commission; SEC Section 31, FINRA TAF and CAT pass-through at the highest rates in force 2022-01..2026-11, each rounded up to USD 0.01 per order).
- Base fills: half-spread 1.0 bps, slippage 1.0 bps, participation 0.10 of bar volume, latency 500 ms, simulated missed entries 0%.
- Stressed fills: half-spread 2.0 bps, slippage 2.0 bps, participation 0.10 of bar volume, latency 500 ms, simulated missed entries 5%.
- Entries (daily bars, positions carried overnight): decided on a completed session's close; a marketable limit at the estimated ask plus the rule's frozen offset (T-0013 5 bps, at most 10 sessions, target is a cost gate only; T-0014 100 bps, at most 260 sessions), filled only at the next session's open within the limit, else missed. Exit signals and time exits sell at the next open; a stop rests for the whole hold, is checked before the target in the same bar, and an open through it sells at that open (STOP_GAP). The target needs the estimated bid strictly through it. The signal bar's close is never a fill price.
- Risk at USD 50 (shared policy): planned loss per trade <= 0.125 USD including both fees and a 10 bps gap allowance; <= 10 USD per symbol, <= 20 USD gross, >= 30 USD cash reserve; at most 2 new entries per NY session and one pending entry; daily 0.50 USD and weekly 1.25 USD loss pauses, 10% drawdown and the USD 45 floor.
- Research assumptions: every 3-month window is an independent deployment from a fresh USD 50 ledger (bars of the 260 preceding exchange sessions feed the features only; no decision before the window is traded; a position still open at the window end is closed at the last bar's estimated bid). Inside a window a daily/weekly loss pause is treated as reviewed at the next loss window, and a drawdown or floor pause stops new entries until the window ends.
- Bar data cannot resolve sub-bar order: these results are preliminary strategy research and do not certify execution realism (risk_and_validation.md §6).

## 12. Bias and limitations

- Universe (declared at registration): Fixed on 2026-11-09 by the shared daily-bar design before any ETF data was downloaded or inspected. The 16 funds are SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA, the nine original Select Sector SPDRs (XLB, XLE, XLF, XLI, XLK, XLP, XLU, XLV, XLY), TLT, IEF and GLD: large, liquid, unleveraged US-listed funds that traded throughout 2016-2026. This is today's list applied to past history, not a point-in-time universe. Survivorship bias among broad index and sector funds is small but not zero: the list was chosen knowing these funds survived and stayed liquid. The sector line-up also changed. XLRE (2015) and XLC (2018) are excluded, and the September 2018 sector reclassification moved large companies from XLK and XLY into the new XLC, so a sector fund is not a constant basket. T-0013 trades the 13 equity ETFs, as Connors and Alvarez tested equities and equity ETFs; T-0014 trades all 16, Faber's multi-asset idea. The funds overlap heavily: SPY, QQQ, IWM and DIA hold largely the same large-cap stocks, and the sector funds partition the S&P 500. Sixteen funds are therefore far fewer independent bets. Exposure tags (ADR-0015) cap SPY, QQQ, IWM and DIA together at the 20% symbol cap (US_EQUITY_INDEX); TLT and IEF share US_TREASURY; GLD is GOLD; sector funds are untagged. US ETFs are research-only: the STOCKS profile admits ordinary shares only, and whether the owner's account may buy US ETFs (product-document rules for European retail investors, markets_and_brokers.md section 7) is unconfirmed.
- Entry, exit and feed (declared at registration): Decision at a completed daily bar (session close + 1 s). The order is a next-open marketable buy limit: the estimated ask at the signal close plus the rule's frozen offset (T-0013 5 bps, T-0014 100 bps). It fills only at the next session's opening price plus the assumed half-spread and slippage, and only if that price is within the limit. Otherwise the entry is missed: there is no later fill that day, and a missing bar at that open is also a missed entry. The published rules trade at the close: Connors and Alvarez buy and sell on the close, and Faber trades the month-end close. Here every order is placed after the close and fills at the next open, so overnight gaps are borne. T-0013's 5 bps offset acts as a no-gap-up filter that the book does not have: an open more than about 3 bps above the signal close misses the entry. T-0014's 1% collar admits ordinary opening gaps. Exits. Exit signals (T-0013: a close above SMA(5); T-0014: a month-end close below its 10-month average) and time exits sell at the next open. Disaster stops rest for the whole hold; they are not in the published rules, but the policy needs one to size a trade. Inside a bar the stop is checked before the target, and an open through the stop sells at that open (STOP_GAP); overnight gaps can exceed the 10 bps gap allowance. T-0013's target (SMA(5) at the signal) is only a cost-coverage gate, never an exit. T-0014's 3R target is a resting exit. Policy. One pending entry at a time, at most 2 new entries per session and at most 2 open positions. When several ETFs signal at the same close, the first in instrument-id order is taken. This is not the equal-weight portfolios of the publications. Data. Alpaca SIP consolidated daily bars at raw prices. Open, high, low and close are regular-hours prices; the open is the first qualifying trade, not necessarily the opening auction. Volume and VWAP include 04:00-20:00 New York trades and are used only for the participation cap. Distributions are neither credited nor adjusted, so ex-dividend drops count as losses and enter RSI, SMA and ATR. This understates total return most for TLT and IEF, which pay monthly. The SIP feed also differs from the IEX single-venue bars of the stock baseline and the paper runtime. Costs. Alpaca charges no commission on US-listed stocks and ETFs. SEC Section 31, FINRA TAF and CAT are assumed to apply to ETF trades as to shares, and the 2022-2026 maximum rates of configs/fees/alpaca_us_equities_v2.yaml are assumed to bound 2017-2021. Both assumptions are to be confirmed with dated sources before registration. Fractional day orders follow the stock route; their acceptance at the open is unverified until the paper certification run.
- Bar-only replay: no quotes, queue position or intrabar path; the spread is an assumption rather than a measurement, and passive (limit-resting) execution is not modelled.
- Pessimistic intrabar sequencing: when one bar touches both the stop and the target, the stop is assumed (never target-first). Zero-commission runs: T-0013 0 of 249 trades; T-0014 0 of 91 trades were decided this way, so this bias is small. (Stops inside the entry bar are not an assumption: the entry fills at that bar's open, so its low comes after the entry.)
- Costs: Alpaca US equities via API - zero commission; SEC Section 31, FINRA TAF and CAT pass-through at the highest rates in force 2022-01..2026-11, each rounded up to USD 0.01 per order. Taxes are not modelled; one USD account currency is assumed.
- Loss-pause reviews are assumed at the next window; a real owner might pause longer.
- The walk-forward series is short relative to the G2 floor of 120 effective sessions and 100 trades unless stated otherwise above; uncertainty intervals are wide.
