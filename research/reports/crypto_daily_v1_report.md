## 3. Execution and cost assumptions

- Fees: `kraken-pro-spot-tier1-usd-2026-11-06` (Kraken Pro Tier 1: 0.40% maker / 0.80% taker, documented 2026-11-06; marketable orders pay taker on both legs; rounded up to 0.0001).
- Base fills: half-spread 1.0 bps, slippage 2.0 bps, participation 0.10 of bar volume, latency 500 ms, simulated missed entries 0%.
- Stressed fills: half-spread 2.0 bps, slippage 4.0 bps, participation 0.10 of bar volume, latency 500 ms, simulated missed entries 5%.
- Entries (daily bars, positions carried overnight): decided on a completed session's close; a marketable limit at the estimated ask plus the rule's frozen offset (T-0016 100 bps, at most 365 sessions), filled only at the next session's open within the limit, else missed. Exit signals and time exits sell at the next open; a stop rests for the whole hold, is checked before the target in the same bar, and an open through it sells at that open (STOP_GAP). The target needs the estimated bid strictly through it. The signal bar's close is never a fill price.
- Risk at USD 50 (shared policy): planned loss per trade <= 0.125 USD including both fees and a 10 bps gap allowance; <= 10 USD per symbol, <= 20 USD gross, >= 30 USD cash reserve; at most 2 new entries per UTC day and one pending entry; daily 0.50 USD and weekly 1.25 USD loss pauses, 10% drawdown and the USD 45 floor.
- Research assumptions: every 3-month window is an independent deployment from a fresh USD 50 ledger (bars of the 365 preceding UTC days feed the features only; no decision before the window is traded; a position still open at the window end is closed at the last bar's estimated bid). Inside a window a daily/weekly loss pause is treated as reviewed at the next loss window, and a drawdown or floor pause stops new entries until the window ends.
- Bar data cannot resolve sub-bar order: these results are preliminary strategy research and do not certify execution realism (risk_and_validation.md §6).

## 12. Bias and limitations

- Fixed two-symbol universe chosen by specification, not point-in-time membership; results do not generalise to other assets.
- Coinbase prices proxy for Kraken execution; the measured basis is small but non-zero and was observed over one recent week only.
- Bar-only replay: no quotes, queue position or intrabar path; passive (maker) execution is therefore not modelled, and taker fees apply to every fill.
- Pessimistic intrabar sequencing: when one bar touches both the stop and the target, the stop is assumed (never target-first). Zero-commission runs: T-0016 0 of 33 trades were decided this way, so this bias is small. (Stops inside the entry bar are not an assumption: the entry fills at that bar's open, so its low comes after the entry.)
- One USD account currency assumed; EUR pairs, FX and the owner's actual fee tier are unknown. Taxes are not modelled.
- Loss-pause reviews are assumed at the next window; a real owner might pause longer.
- The walk-forward series is short relative to the G2 floor of 120 effective sessions and 100 trades unless stated otherwise above; uncertainty intervals are wide.
