# Cost feasibility at USD 50

Status: M0 deliverable (instructions.md §6.3; implementation_roadmap.md §2). Prepared 2026-11-07.

This document answers one question: **can a USD 50 account place and close the planned trades at a
cost that leaves any room for a net edge?** Every number inside a `GENERATED` block is computed by
`trader docs build` from:

- the canonical policy (`configs/policy/base.yaml`, which mirrors risk_and_validation.md §1);
- the fee schedules (`configs/fees/*.yaml`);
- the sizing engine (`src/trader/risk/sizing.py`).

`tests/test_demo_and_cli.py::test_generated_documentation_is_current` fails if a table here goes stale.
Fee schedules are dated snapshots. They must be re-fetched for the actual account before any paper or live use.

> Nothing here is a forecast or evidence of profitability. It shows what costs and limits allow.
> It does not show what a strategy will earn.

What the table shows:

- **IBKR fractional fees.** The rule is 1% with a USD 0.01 minimum, so a round trip costs about 2% of notional. That is about USD 0.20 on a USD 10 position, already more than the whole per-trade risk budget. The replay `costly` scenario confirms it: every candidate is rejected `TOO_COSTLY` (see `runs/demo/costly/report.md` after `trader demo`).
- **Kraken Tier 1 taker fees.** A taker round trip costs 1.6% (USD 0.16 on USD 10), which is also more than the USD 0.125 budget. This matches CRYPTO_TRADING_TRACK.md §6.
- **Alpaca.** `alpaca-us-equities-unverified` (`configs/fees/alpaca_us_equities.yaml`) is kept unchanged as the 2026-11-07 record: unknown, so `NO_TRADE`. On 2026-11-08 the fees were verified from official pages, and `configs/fees/alpaca_us_equities_v2.yaml` (`alpaca-us-equities-conservative-2026-11-08`) is the documented research cost basis; each source is listed in that file with what it says.
  - [Alpaca Brokerage Fee Schedule](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/BrokFeeSched.pdf) (revised 2026-10-12): no commission on ordinary US equity trades (commissions apply only to index options, the Elite Smart Router and non-retail order flow). Pass-through fees: SEC Section 31 on sells (0.0000206 × trade value today), FINRA TAF on sells (USD 0 per share today, see below) and the FINRA CAT fee on buys **and** sells (USD 0.000003 per share today). Fees are computed on the exact executed quantity, fractional shares included; each fee type is aggregated per account per day and rounded up to the cent. The [support article](https://alpaca.markets/support/regulatory-fees) (February 2026) describes the SEC fee and the TAF as rounded up to the penny per trade; the [fractional-trading page](https://docs.alpaca.markets/docs/fractional-trading) says fractional fees "work the same way as with whole shares".
  - SEC Section 31 rate, per [SEC orders](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/fee-rate-advisories): USD 5.10 per million until 2022-05-13, 22.90 from 2022-05-14, 8.00 from 2023-02-27, **27.80 from 2024-05-22** (the maximum), 0.00 from 2025-05-14, 20.60 from 2026-04-04.
  - FINRA TAF on covered equity sales (SR-FINRA-2020-032, [SR-FINRA-2024-019](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rule-filings/sr-finra-2024-019/fee-adjustment-schedule)): USD 0.000130 per share (max 6.49) in 2022, 0.000145 (max 7.27) in 2023, 0.000166 (max 8.30) in 2024-2025, **0.000195 (max 9.79) in 2026** (the maximum); paused at 0 for 2026-10-01 .. 2026-12-31 ([SR-FINRA-2026-021](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2026-09/SR-FINRA-2026-021.pdf)).
  - CAT industry-member fees ([CAT fee alerts](https://www.catnmsplan.com/cat-fee-alerts)): at most USD 0.000048 per share (CAT Fee 2024-1 plus Historical CAT Assessment 1, August 2024 .. January 2025 invoices).
  - The research schedule uses those maxima for the whole 2022-2026 backtest and rounds each fee type up to the cent on **every order**. That is an upper bound of Alpaca's daily aggregation: a tiny buy costs USD 0.01 (CAT) and a tiny sell USD 0.03 (SEC + TAF + CAT), USD 0.04 per round trip, 40 bps of a USD 10 position. Today's daily-aggregated cost would be lower. Assumed, not verified: a retail account trading through the API, and Alpaca passing through the official rates in past periods.
- **Replay demonstrator.** Its fee schedule is a project assumption: zero commission, plus SEC and FINRA sell-side fees, each rounded up to the cent. It is there to exercise the software, not to describe a real broker.

What the table shows:

- **Zero-commission route.** With a tight stop, the USD 10 symbol cap (20%) is the binding constraint. With wider stops, the per-trade risk budget binds. For example, a 2.5% stop leaves roughly USD 4 of notional. In every case the regulatory sell fee minimum (USD 0.01-0.02) is a large fraction of any realistic intraday gross move on USD 4-10.
- **IBKR route.** Positions are cut to about USD 2.5-4.2, and the move needed to cover costs is above 2%. That is not a plausible intraday edge.
- **Alpaca, conservative documented fees.** The USD 0.04 round trip leaves USD 3.3-10 of notional and needs a gross move of 0.44-1.26% before any edge, more than the replay demonstrator's assumption because the CAT fee is charged on both legs.

What the table shows:

- **Taker execution.** It is infeasible at USD 10, and at USD 5 with a 1% stop. Kraken's actual minimum order sizes per pair are still unknown; they must be read from `AssetPairs` metadata.
- **Maker-only execution.** It may fit the budget on paper, but it needs a conservative fill model: queue position, missed fills and adverse selection. That model is part of the crypto replay work.

**Conclusion:** the crypto track stays in research and replay. Any live crypto pilot is blocked until the actual account fee tier, minimum sizes and a validated maker fill model all exist.

## 7. Feasibility verdict (G0 inputs)

| Route | Verdict on 2026-11-07 | Binding blockers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US fractional equities, zero-commission broker | **Technically plausible; economically unproven** | Alpaca fees are documented for research since 2026-11-08 (conservative USD 0.04 per round trip, section 2) but not confirmed for the owner's account; account-rule profile, fractional limit/protective order support, Germany eligibility and funding route are all unverified |
| US fractional equities via IBKR | **Uneconomic at USD 50** | About 2% round-trip commission; fractional API support also unresolved |
| BTC/ETH spot via Kraken | **Uneconomic with taker fills**; maker-only unproven | Account fee tier, pair minimums, maker fill model, UAT access |
| Gold/silver CFDs | **NO_TRADE at USD 50** | Minimum contract notional versus the 20% symbol cap and 0.25% risk budget; instrument metadata not fetched |
| ASX gold miners | **Not feasible at USD 50** | A$500 marketable parcel; A$2,500-4,000+ equity implied by the risk limits (markets_and_brokers.md §5) |

G0 is **not passed**. The only route worth pursuing to paper (M4) is zero-commission US fractional equities, and only after the owner confirms the items in `docs/owner_setup.md` §4.
